# Verification guide

- `CLAIMS.md` records the public mathematical and computational claim status.
- `CLAIM-TO-ARTIFACT-MAP.md` maps paper claims to reproducible evidence.
- `../BLIND_CHECK.md` documents the separate reconstruction scripts.
- `bash run_all.sh` executes the maintained deterministic release gate.
- `../code/test_fail_closed.py` exercises the release gates with adversarial
  counterexamples and malformed proof state.
- `../code/test_certificate_contracts.py` checks width, resume replay, interval
  parsing, geometric radius coverage, tail-base margins and evidence bindings.

The fast verifier checks report schemas, underlying predicates, exact expected
component sets, configuration and witness identity, partition closure, and all
cross-report content bindings. The complete Arb replay reports are shipped and
bound to every compact leaf and fixed-radius angular direction.

The repository checks support the scoped claims listed in these files. They do
not substitute for journal peer review.

The fast gate validates the retained full-replay reports; it does not execute the
full spatial workflows. The tail transfer supports the exact packaged witness at
R=582 with 200,000 angular directions, and requires both point-tail records to
establish at least 0.0273. Other witnesses need their own proven base context.

The shipped Decimal reports preserve historical bytes. Exact compatibility
anchors bind those records to their configurations and leaf exports. Their
numerical upper/lower labels are diagnostic, not proof-bearing bounds. New
producers emit explicit context-bound diagnostic schemas; primary interval and
complete Arb checks remain mandatory. Full regeneration can change report bytes
and requires validating new bindings before freezing a successor manifest.
